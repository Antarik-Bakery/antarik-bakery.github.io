Good to know

Your questions,
answered.

Are your cakes homemade?

Yes. Antarik Bakery is a home-based bakery in Falakata, Alipurduar. We make our cakes at home with care.

Do you offer eggless cakes?

Yes, eggless cakes are available as an option. Please mention your preference when you contact us about your cake.

How much do your cakes cost?

Our cakes start from ₹150. The final price depends on the size, flavour and design. Contact us for a quote for your chosen cake.

Can I customise a birthday or anniversary cake?

Yes. We make custom cakes for birthdays, anniversaries and other celebrations. Tell us your preferred colours, theme, name, message or design idea, and we'll discuss the options with you.

How do I place an order?

Call or WhatsApp us with your occasion, date and cake ideas. Your order is confirmed once we have agreed on the design, price, date and collection or delivery arrangements.

Where is Antarik Bakery located?

We are based in Falakata, Alipurduar, West Bengal. Contact us for our exact address and to arrange cake collection.
